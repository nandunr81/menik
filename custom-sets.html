Our process

How we make your set

  1. 01

    Your brief

    Tell us the design, the centre stone shape and size, how many accents you need and your budget per set.

  2. 02

    Sourcing and cutting

    We source the centre stone and accents in Sri Lanka, or cut them to the sizes your design needs.

  3. 03

    Matching

    The accents are matched with each other for colour, cut and size, and checked against the centre stone.

  4. 04

    Photos and quote

    You receive photos and videos of the stones we propose, with the price for the whole set.

  5. 05

    Approve, pay and ship

    Confirm the set and pay before dispatch. The stones are packed together and shipped tracked and insured.

Origin and any treatment, including heating of white sapphires, are stated on every quote and invoice.
