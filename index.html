FAQ

Common questions

Is there a minimum order?+

Minimums are low. You can start with a small parcel to test colour and cut before you commit to volume.

Do you state treatment and origin?+

Yes. Origin and any treatment, including heating of white sapphires, are stated on every quote and invoice. Lab reports are available for larger stones on request.

How do I pay?+

Bank transfer or a secure payment platform. New buyers pay before dispatch.

How are stones shipped?+

Door-to-door by courier with a tracking number, insured, with export paperwork and a commercial invoice included.

What if the stones do not match the quote?+

There is a return window for stones that do not match the quote. You also see photos and videos of the actual parcel before you pay.

Can you cut a size that is not in the chart?+

Yes. Sizes that are not listed can be cut to order. Send us the shape, size and colour you need.
